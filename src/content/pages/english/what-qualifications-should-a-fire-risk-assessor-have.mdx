# What Qualifications Should a Fire Risk Assessor Have?

Choosing the right person to carry out a fire risk assessment is important for the safety of your building, staff, visitors and residents. A qualified fire risk assessor should have the right training, knowledge, practical experience and professional competence to identify fire hazards and recommend suitable safety measures.

## What Are Fire Risk Assessor Qualifications?

There is no qualification that alone makes you the right fit for each kind of building at all. The assessor needs to have skills and experience commensurate with the size, use and complexities of the premises.

The current fire risk assessment competency requirements are supported by BS 8674:2025, which sets out three levels of competence: Foundation, Intermediate and Advanced. These levels reflect the type and risk of the building being assessed.

A good fire risk assessor training programme should cover areas such as fire hazards, fire prevention, escape routes, fire doors, emergency procedures and the use of relevant fire safety guidance.

## What Makes Someone a Competent Fire Risk Assessor?

An experienced fire risk assessor should understand the building, foresee possible fire risks and determine if existing fire safety measures are adequate or appropriate. Whilst qualifications do of course matter, as the adage goes, experience and professional judgement also count for something.

Depending on the property, an assessor may need knowledge of fire alarms, emergency lighting, fire doors, evacuation procedures and other fire protection measures. ## Does Fire Risk Assessor Certification Matter?

Yes. When choosing an assessor, it is sensible to check their fire risk assessor certification, professional experience and evidence of competence. Fire risk assessor accreditation and recognised professional schemes can provide additional confidence that an assessor follows established standards.
