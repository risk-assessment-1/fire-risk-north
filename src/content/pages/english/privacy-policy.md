Fire Risk North ("we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

## Who We Are

We are the data controller for personal data collected through this website and in the course of providing our services. Registered address: Bartle House, Oxford Court, Manchester M2 3WQ. Telephone: +44 161 987 1020. Email: enquiries@firerisknorth.com.

## Information We Collect

We collect only the information we need:

- **Contact details** you give us through the contact form, by phone or by email — for example your name, phone number, email address and company details.
- **Site and service information** needed to deliver our services, such as the address of premises being assessed and any correspondence or documents you share with us.
- **Technical data** about how you use this website, such as pages visited, through cookies and analytics (see Cookies below).

## How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data to:

- respond to enquiries and provide quotes (legitimate interests or steps preparatory to a contract);
- deliver fire risk assessments and related services, including scheduling visits and issuing reports (performance of a contract);
- meet legal, accounting and regulatory obligations (legal obligation);
- send you service-related communications, such as report delivery or appointment reminders (performance of a contract);
- occasionally send marketing updates, but only if you have asked us to or opted in (consent). You can unsubscribe at any time.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

## Cookies

This website uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, to understand how the site is used and to improve it. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; doing so will not prevent you from using the site.

## Data Sharing

We share personal data only with service providers who help us operate our business (for example IT hosting, email and payment processors), each bound by contractual confidentiality obligations, or where we are required to by law. Where a service is delivered in partnership with an assessor or consultant, we share only the information necessary to deliver that service.

## Data Retention

We keep enquiry data for up to 24 months and client records (including assessments and invoices) for up to 6 years, in line with limitation periods and professional requirements. After that, data is securely deleted or anonymised.

## Your Rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

- request a copy of the personal data we hold about you (data subject access request);
- ask us to correct inaccurate data;
- ask us to delete your data in certain circumstances;
- object to or restrict our processing, including direct marketing;
- request a transfer of your data to another provider (data portability);
- withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, email enquiries@firerisknorth.com. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.

## Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections, access controls and secure storage. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we review our safeguards regularly.

## International Transfers

Your data is generally stored within the UK. Where any provider processes data outside the UK, we ensure appropriate safeguards (such as UK adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses) are in place.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page, with significant changes highlighted on the website.

## Contact Us

For any privacy questions or requests, contact enquiries@firerisknorth.com or write to Fire Risk North, Bartle House, Oxford Court, Manchester M2 3WQ.
