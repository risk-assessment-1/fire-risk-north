# How Much Does a Fire Risk Assessment Cost in the UK?

In the UK, the cost of a fire risk assessment depends on the size, type, and complexity of the property. A large warehouse, high-rise building or other complex premises may require a more detailed assessment than a small office or simple commercial property. Other factors influencing the final price could include number of occupants, escape routes, fire safety systems and building layout.

## What Affects Fire Risk Assessment Pricing?

There is no single fire risk assessment price that applies to every property. Before giving a quote, an assessor has to examine the building in order to understand risks. Things that may impact price include:

- Size and layout of the property
- Number of people using the building
- Type of business or property
- Number of floors and escape routes
- Fire alarms and detection systems
- Fire doors and emergency lighting
- Firefighting equipment
- Presence of vulnerable occupants
- Level of fire risk and building complexity

For this reason, the commercial fire risk assessment cost for a small office may be very different from the cost of assessing a warehouse, hotel, care home or high-rise building.

The condition of fire doors can also influence the scope of an assessment. Where required, additional services such as a [fire door inspection](/services/fire-door-inspection) can help identify issues with fire doors, including damage, gaps, faulty hardware or problems that could affect their performance. More detailed [fire door surveying](/services/fire-door-surveying) may also be appropriate for buildings with multiple fire doors or more complex fire safety requirements.
