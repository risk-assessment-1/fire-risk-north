# What Happens During a Fire Risk Assessment?

A fire risk assessment process is a detailed check of a building to identify fire hazards and make sure people can leave safely if a fire occurs. It looks at possible sources of fire, people who may be at risk, and the fire safety measures already in place. Risk Assessment North provides professional [fire risk assessments](/services/fire-risk-assessment) for commercial and residential properties, using a structured approach aligned with PAS 79 and UK fire safety requirements.

## What Happens During a Fire Risk Assessment?

Generally, the fire risk assessment inspection begins with a property visit. The qualified assessor scans the building for all areas that could impede fire safety. This covers offices, corridors, stairways, kitchens, storage areas, plant rooms and more.

The assessor looks for common fire hazards such as electrical equipment, heating systems, cooking equipment, flammable materials and anything that could increase the chance of a fire starting or spreading.

## The Main Fire Risk Assessment Steps

The fire risk assessment steps are designed to give a clear picture of the building's current fire safety condition.

The assessment also checks important safety measures such as:

- Fire alarms and detection systems
- Emergency lighting
- Fire doors
- Escape routes and emergency exits
- Fire extinguishers and other firefighting equipment
- Fire safety signs
- Staff awareness and evacuation arrangements

The assessor may also review whether the existing fire safety evacuation planning is suitable for the building and its occupants.

## What Is Included in the Assessment Report?

After the inspection, the assessor records the fire risk assessment findings in a clear report. The report explains the hazards identified, the level of risk, and any improvements that may be needed.

A fire risk assessment checklist keeps a check that no important area goes unnoticed in the inspection process. Recommendations may include fire doors, escape routes, alarms, emergency lighting, staff training or other safety measures depending on the property.

Risk Assessment North provides photographic reports with prioritised remedial actions, helping property owners and responsible persons understand what needs attention.

## Fire Risk Assessment vs Fire Strategy Assessment

A Fire Strategy Assessment and a fire risk assessment have different purposes. A fire risk assessment looks at the existing condition and risks within an occupied building. A [fire strategy](/services/fire-strategy) is generally used during building design, construction, refurbishment or change of use to plan fire safety measures.
