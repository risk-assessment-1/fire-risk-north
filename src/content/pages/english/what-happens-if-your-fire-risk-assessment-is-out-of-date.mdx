The laws of fire safety also include a twist, as there is no deadline for your [fire risk assessment](/services/fire-risk-assessment) report, but it may become invalid instantly. Basically, it comes down to how relevant your report is with regard to the current usage of the building.

If your [office fire risk assessment](/services/fire-risk-assessment/offices) report is outdated, you should go through the building with this report and make all the needed changes. If the building itself or its usage has changed greatly, you should make a completely new [fire risk assessment for flats](/services/fire-risk-assessment/flats).

## Does A Fire Risk Assessment Have A Validity Date?

No, the law does not specify an expiration date for an assessment or a fixed duration of its validity; thus, the idea of the validity of a fire risk assessment for three years is a myth. Article 9 requires regular reviews, and there should be other reviews if the overdue fire safety assessment is no longer valid or if there are changes in the premises.

## Why Does The Assessment Get Out-Of-Date?

- Alterations made in the building in the form of renovations, partitioning, escape route changes, etc.
- Changes in the usage or an increase in the number of people who will need assistance while evacuating the building.
- Introduction of new machinery, processes or equipment in the building, including the charging station for e-bikes and other tools.
- Any fire, near-miss situation or any recommendations from the previous assessment that were not carried out.

That last equipment point cuts both ways: new machinery or e-bike charging can change the fire profile so much that your detection needs review too — our guide to [how often a commercial fire alarm should be tested and serviced](/how-often-is-a-commercial-fire-alarm-tested-serviced) explains when a system should be re-examined.

## What Should We Do At Present?

The first step is to make a note of all the changes in the building from the last assessment and walk through the building holding the report, noting all the changes, and see if any action from the previous expired fire risk assessment has been undertaken. While you walk, check that routine cover did not lapse in the gap — monthly extinguisher checks, for instance, as set out in our guide to [how often commercial fire extinguishers need servicing](/how-often-fire-extinguishers-need-servicing).

A suitable assessor should also be selected for complicated properties. Use our [questions to ask before hiring a fire safety company](/questions-to-ask-before-hiring-fire-safety-company) before you commission the review.

## What Has To Be Documented?

Article 9 mandates a report on each fire risk assessment renewal or fire safety compliance review, and since 1 October 2023, it is necessary for every responsible person. The findings and actions carried out or planned, along with the high-risk group of people, should be noted down — the same prioritised actions that follow any fresh assessment, as explained in [what happens after a fire risk assessment identifies problems](/what-happens-after-a-fire-risk-assessment).

## What Is The Risk Of Not Getting It Done?

There are penalties from fire and rescue authorities that could include an enforcement or prohibition notice, with offences that put lives at risk being punishable by unlimited fines and up to two years' imprisonment.
