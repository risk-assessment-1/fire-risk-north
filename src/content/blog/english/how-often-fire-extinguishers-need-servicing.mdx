A fire extinguisher may look ready to use, but its condition cannot be judged by appearance alone. Regular visual checks and professional servicing help ensure it remains safe and operational. With proper understanding of the procedures involved in checking, servicing, and overhauling the device, the otherwise overlooked device becomes a reliable one.

Monthly checks and yearly maintenance are the actions that need to be taken in relation to any fire extinguisher used commercially. However, apart from this, extinguishers such as water, foam, powder, and wet chemical extinguishers have to be maintained every five years, while CO₂ extinguishers have to be maintained every ten years.

## What Should The Monthly Inspection Include?

Under BS 5306-3, the responsible person should arrange a visual inspection of fire extinguishers at least once a month. All fire extinguisher inspections must be done to make sure that they are available and not damaged; that they are fitted with the correct pin and tamper seal; and that the green gauge works well.

Pair the monthly extinguisher check with the [weekly commercial fire alarm test](/blog/how-often-is-a-commercial-fire-alarm-tested-serviced) so that one simple routine covers both.

## How Often Do They Need To Be Serviced?

An experienced engineer will service all the extinguishers annually and will attach a servicing label together with the report. A very good way to show the certification of the company to perform such service is BAFE SP101. Professional [fire extinguisher servicing](/services/fire-extinguisher-servicing) is also important for the [office fire risk assessment](/services/fire-risk-assessment/offices), where the assessor will expect labels to be in date.

## What Is Included In Extended Service And Overhaul?

Extended service includes the removal and inspection of the unit followed by refilling, and it is performed every five years for the water-based and powder units. The cylinders in CO₂ are highly pressurised, so they go through an overhaul after ten years, along with pressure tests. This is important in relation to the [warehouse fire risk assessment](/services/fire-risk-assessment/warehouses), where extinguisher numbers and types are checked in detail.

## When Do You Need Replacement?

There is no such UK standard which states the fire extinguisher replacement time, and it will depend on the condition of the unit. There have been certain conditions mentioned by BS 5306-3, according to which the extinguisher could be useless, and some of these conditions are corrosion, denting, and gouging, among others, including those identified during a [restaurant fire risk assessment](/services/fire-risk-assessment/restaurants).

## Is It A Legal Requirement To Service?

Servicing is a legal requirement according to the Fire Safety Order, which stipulates that firefighting equipment should be kept in good order. BS 5306-3 outlines the fire extinguisher maintenance requirements. Servicing is also a way to prove to the authorities and to the insurance company that you comply, but it all depends on your situation. Proper commercial fire extinguisher servicing frequency is also important to avoid any accident.

Extinguisher upkeep is one of the [corrective actions that follow a fire risk assessment](/blog/what-happens-after-a-fire-risk-assessment), and keeping equipment in efficient working order goes hand in hand with knowing [what to do if your fire risk assessment is out of date](/blog/what-happens-if-your-fire-risk-assessment-is-out-of-date).
