faq_section:
  enable: true
  heading: Frequently Asked Questions About Fire Risk Assessments in the North West
  description: Questions we hear most often from businesses, landlords and property managers across the region.
  items:
    - question: Are fire risk assessments a legal requirement?
      answer: Yes. Under the Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005, anyone responsible for non-domestic premises or the common parts of residential buildings must hold a suitable and sufficient fire risk assessment. That duty applies across the North West, from single offices to large HMOs.
    - question: How often are fire risk assessments required?
      answer: There is no fixed expiry in law, but assessments must be reviewed whenever the building, its use or its occupants change materially. Annual review is standard practice for most landlords and insurers, with a full reassessment every three to four years.
    - question: Who is responsible for arranging a fire risk assessment in the North West?
      answer: The responsible person under the Fire Safety Order. In practice that means the employer for a workplace, and the landlord, managing agent or residents' management company for the communal parts of a residential building.
    - question: How long does a risk assessment take?
      answer: A small shop or single-floor office can be finished in under an hour. A multi-storey hotel, a large HMO or a mixed-use block takes longer, sometimes most of a day. Size, layout, occupancy and the number of separate risk areas drive the timing.
    - question: How is the fire risk assessment carried out?
      answer: On site, following PAS 79 methodology. The assessor walks the building, inspects alarms, fire doors, escape routes, emergency lighting and extinguishers, reviews records where they exist and speaks to whoever manages the premises before writing up the findings.
    - question: What documentation do I receive after the assessment?
      answer: A written fire risk assessment report setting out the premises and scope, the hazards and people at risk, the evaluation of existing precautions, significant findings, prioritised recommendations and guidance on review. It is the fire safety documentation insurers, licensing teams and enforcing authorities ask to see.
    - question: Can you help implement the recommendations in the report?
      answer: Yes. Fire alarm testing, fire door inspection, fire extinguisher servicing and emergency light testing are all services we provide, so the actions in your report can be closed out by the same team that wrote it.
    - question: Do you have a fire risk assessment template?
      answer: We do not hand out blank templates, because a self-completed form rarely satisfies an enforcing officer. Instead we produce the completed, compliant report itself, structured to PAS 79 and signed off by a BAFE SP205 registered assessor.
    - question: How often should a fire risk assessment be reviewed?
      answer: Review annually as a baseline, and immediately after a refurbishment, change of use, new tenant or change of occupancy. Any incident or near miss should also trigger a fresh look at the assessment.
    - question: How much does a fire risk assessment cost?
      answer: Assessments start from £249 for smaller properties. The final figure depends on the size of the premises, the number of floors, the building type, occupancy levels and how much documentation is involved. We quote on the details rather than a flat rate.
    - question: Do I need a fire risk assessment?
      answer: If you control non-domestic premises, or the common areas of a residential building such as a block of flats or an HMO, then yes. That covers offices, shops, hotels, guest houses, warehouses, care homes, schools and similar premises across the North West.
    - question: Do you provide other fire safety services?
      answer: Beyond the assessment we cover fire alarm testing, emergency light testing, fire door inspection and surveying, fire extinguisher servicing and fire strategy work, so one team can keep a building compliant end to end.
